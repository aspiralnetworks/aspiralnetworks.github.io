Aspiral Networks Private Limited operates Yard (YardStore and YardBuy), a platform connecting Buyers with local Sellers. We take complaints seriously. Buyers pay Sellers directly, so most order problems are first resolved with the Seller, and our Grievance Officer steps in where that does not work or where the complaint is about the platform itself. Complaints are accepted free of charge.

1. What You Can Complain About

  • An Order: late, wrong, damaged or missing items, refusal to refund or return, or overcharging.
  • A Seller's conduct, a misleading Listing, or a prohibited or unlawful Listing.
  • Abusive or fraudulent messages or attachments in chat.
  • Unwanted promotional notifications.
  • Membership points that were not credited, wrongly deducted or not honoured.
  • Problems with the apps or website, including account and verification issues.
  • Billing issues with a Seller subscription.
  • Content that is unlawful, infringes your rights or impersonates you.
  • Your personal data (see Section 8).

2. Step 1: Contact the Seller or Support

For an Order problem, message the Seller in the Order chat or use "Help" on the Order. For general help, write to yard-services@spiralnetworks.in. If it is not resolved, or you prefer not to go through these, go straight to the Grievance Officer. You do not have to complete Step 1 first for complaints about unlawful content, data misuse or harassment.

4. What to Include

  • Your name, registered mobile number and email address, and whether you are a Buyer or a Seller.
  • The Order ID, Seller or Buyer name, and date, if the complaint is about an Order.
  • A clear description of the problem and what resolution you want.
  • Evidence such as photos, videos, screenshots of chats or Listings, or bills.

5. What Happens Next

  • Acknowledgement: we acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours of receiving it and give you a unique complaint reference number to track it.
  • Investigation: we review the details and contact the Seller or Buyer concerned, and may ask you for more information.
  • Resolution: we aim to resolve complaints within 15 days. If a matter is complex, we will tell you why and give an update, and in any case we close it within one month of receiving it.
  • Unlawful content: complaints about content that is intimate or sexual about you, or that impersonates you, are acted on within 24 hours of receiving the complaint.
  • Our reply: we tell you the outcome in writing, with the action taken, for example a refund by the Seller, a warning to or suspension of the Seller, removal of content, or a correction on your account.
  • Records: we keep a log of each complaint and its outcome.

6. If You Are Not Satisfied

  • Review request: reply to our outcome email within 15 days with "Escalation" in the subject and we will have a senior person review it.
  • Consumers: you can call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 or approach the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, including online through the e-Daakhil portal.
  • Content complaints: you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee constituted under the IT Rules, 2021, within 30 days of our decision.
  • Data complaints: you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after first using our grievance process.

7. Complaints by Sellers

Sellers can use the same process to complain about a listing rejection, verification decision, account suspension, a subscription charge, abusive Buyer conduct or any other matter. Please include your shop name and registered mobile number. Sellers on the platform must also appoint their own grievance officer (the shop owner can act as one), give us the officer's name, contact number and designation to display to Buyers, acknowledge Buyer complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within one month, as the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 require.

8. Data & Privacy Requests

To access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, or complain about how your data is handled, write to the Grievance Officer above or use the in-app options and deletion pages. See our Privacy Policy for your rights.
