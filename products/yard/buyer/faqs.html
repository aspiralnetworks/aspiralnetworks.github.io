Yard Buyer App

Buyer FAQs

Find answers to the most common questions about using the Yard Buyer App.

1. Getting Started
Q1. Who can register as a buyer on Yard?

Any individual or customer in India can register and start ordering products online.

Q2. How do I install the Yard Buyer App?

You can download the app from the Downloads page or directly from the Google Play Store / App Store.

Q3. Is there any registration fee?

No, registration is completely free. You only pay for the products you purchase.

2. Account & Verification
Q4. What information is required to create a buyer account?

Basic personal details such as name, mobile number, email, and delivery address are required.

Q5. How do I update my account information?

You can update your profile, contact details, and delivery addresses via the app settings.

Q6. Can my account be suspended?

Yes, for repeated policy violations, fraudulent activity, or misuse of the platform.

3. Orders & Delivery
Q7. How do I place an order?

Place your order and pay the seller when it's delivered or collected.

Q8. How can I track my order?

Real-time order tracking is available in your dashboard within the app.

Q9. What if my order is delayed?

Contact the seller in the order chat, then use Help.

4. Payments & Refunds
Q10. What payment methods are accepted?

You pay the seller directly, usually in cash or UPI. Confirm with the seller in the order chat.

Q11. Can I cancel an order?

Before the seller accepts; after that, ask the seller in chat.

Q12. How are refunds processed?

The seller refunds you directly. Report problems within 24 hours of delivery or pickup. If it isn't resolved, contact our Grievance Officer.

5. Fees & Charges
Q13. Are there any platform fees?

No, buyers pay Yard nothing.

Q14. Are there hidden charges?

Yard charges buyers nothing. The price on your order is what you pay the seller.

6. Support & Contact
Q15. How do I contact support?

Use the Help & Support section within the app or email yard-services@spiralnetworks.in.

Q16. How can I report issues with a seller?

Link: Grievance Redressal.

7. Points, Chat, Notifications & Complaints
Q17. How do membership points work?

Some sellers run their own membership points. At checkout the app shows what you earn, the minimum order value to earn points, what each point is worth in rupees, and the maximum points you can use on one order. Points work only with the seller that gave them, have no cash value, and are adjusted if you cancel or return an order.

Q18. Can I chat with the seller?

Yes. Open your order and use the chat to message the seller and share photos or files about that order. Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords in chat. Chats are kept for a limited period, so save any evidence you need, such as photos of damaged items.

Q19. How do I stop promotional notifications?

Open the app's notification settings and switch promotions off, either for all sellers or for a particular seller. You will still get order updates and security alerts.

Q20. How long do I have to report a problem with an order?

Please report it within 24 hours of delivery or pickup, using Help on the order. This does not affect your legal rights for defects that could not reasonably be found in 24 hours. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Q21. Will I get an invoice?

Yes. Yard generates an invoice for each completed order on the seller's behalf and emails it to your registered email address.

Q22. How do I contact the Grievance Officer?

Write to grievance.officer@spiralnetworks.in or see the Grievance Redressal page for the officer's details and process. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.
