This notice explains what personal data Aspiral Networks Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us") collects through our website and the Yard Seller App (YardStore) and Yard Buyer App (YardBuy), why we collect it, and the rights you have as a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the rules made under it. By using the Services and giving consent where we ask for it, you agree to the use of your data as described here. Please read it with our Terms & Conditions.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the data we need for the purposes in Section 3.

  • Account data (all users): name, mobile number, email address, login and OTP details, saved delivery addresses.
  • Order data: items ordered, order status, delivery or pickup choice, communication with our support team, reviews and ratings.
  • Chat data: messages, photos, files and other media you send or receive in the in-app chat with a Seller or Buyer about an Order, with time stamps.
  • Membership points data: points earned, redeemed and remaining with each Seller, the Order values they relate to, and the Seller's programme rules.
  • Notification preferences: your choices about promotional push notifications, overall and per Seller.
  • Seller verification data: shop and business details, GST registration (if any) and shop documents, together with the Seller's PAN, Aadhaar number, bank account number and IFSC code, and photos or files of identity documents (such as Aadhaar) that the Seller uploads. We collect these only to verify the Seller's identity and that the shop is genuine, to prevent fraud and to protect Buyers. They are not shown to Buyers, and we do not use bank account details to make or receive payments for Orders.
  • Location: approximate or precise location, only if you allow it, to find nearby Sellers, fill in addresses and verify a Seller's shop location.
  • Photos and files: only those you choose to upload (product images, shop photos, verification documents, complaint evidence, chat attachments).
  • Device and usage data: IP address, device model, operating system, app version, notification token, crash logs and app usage events.
  • From third parties: results from identity or shop verification services, and login details if you sign in with a third-party account.

What we do not collect: card, UPI or bank details for Orders. Buyers pay Sellers directly, so we do not see or hold these payments. Seller subscription payments are processed by our payment gateway provider, which handles the payment details and sends us only the payment confirmation.

3. Why We Use Your Data

We process personal data only for lawful purposes, based on your consent or on legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act (for example where you voluntarily provide data for a purpose, such as placing an Order, or where we must comply with law).

  • To create and manage your account and verify Sellers.
  • To show nearby Sellers and let Buyers place Orders, and to pass Order details to the Seller who must fulfil them.
  • To send Order updates, security alerts and service messages.
  • To handle support requests, complaints and disputes.
  • To enable in-app chat between Buyers and Sellers about Orders, and to review chats and attachments where needed to handle reports, complaints, disputes, fraud or legal requests.
  • To operate Seller membership points programmes by recording points earned and redeemed so the right discount is applied to an Order.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and violations of our Terms.
  • To improve the Services, fix errors and understand how the apps are used.
  • To let Sellers you have ordered from send you promotional push notifications, and to send our own offers, only if you consent.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax, record-keeping and lawful requests from authorities.

4. Your Consent

  • We ask for consent in clear language, separately for each optional purpose such as location access, camera and file access, notifications and marketing. You can use the core Services without the optional ones where technically possible.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time as easily as you gave it, through app or device settings or by writing to us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, but we may no longer be able to provide a feature that depends on that data.
  • Promotional notifications: you receive promotions from a Seller only if you have opted in. You can switch promotions off overall or for a particular Seller in the app's notification settings at any time. Order updates and security alerts are service messages and continue.
  • You may also use a Consent Manager registered under the DPDP Act, once available, to give, manage or withdraw consent.

5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as follows.

  • Sellers and Buyers: a Buyer's name, phone number, delivery address and Order details go to the Seller of that Order, and only to that Seller. Buyers can see Sellers' business name, address, contact details, reviews and listings.
  • Chat: messages and attachments you send in chat are visible to the other party in that conversation, who may save them. We do not read chats routinely. Our authorised staff may access chat content only where a user reports it, to investigate fraud, abuse, safety, a complaint or a dispute, or where the law requires.
  • Membership points: a Seller can see the points balance and history of Buyers within that Seller's own programme, and Buyers see their points with each Seller.
  • Promotional notifications: Sellers can ask us to send promotions to Buyers who have ordered from them and opted in. We deliver them; the Seller does not receive your device identifier.
  • Service providers (processors): hosting and cloud, SMS and notification delivery, mapping (Google Maps and Places for address lookup and navigation), analytics, a payment gateway (for Seller subscription payments), identity and shop verification, and support tools. They act on our instructions under contract and may process data only for those services.
  • Authorities: where required by law, court order or a lawful government request, or to investigate fraud or security incidents.
  • Business transfer: in a merger, acquisition or similar transaction, subject to this policy continuing to apply.

6. Sellers and Your Data

Once a Seller receives a Buyer's details to fulfil an Order, or messages and files you send in chat, the Seller handles that data for its own business and is responsible for it. Our Terms require Sellers to use Buyer data only to fulfil the Order, to keep it secure, not to share or market with it without consent, and to delete it when no longer needed. If a Seller misuses your data, tell us at the address in Section 13.

7. Transfers Outside India

Some service providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We transfer personal data abroad only as permitted by the DPDP Act and not to any country or territory restricted by the Central Government.

8. Retention & Erasure

  • We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, and then delete or anonymise it.
  • Chat messages and attachments are kept for up to 12 months after the Order is completed, and longer only while a complaint or dispute about that Order is open or where the law requires. Points history is kept while the Seller's programme runs and your account is active.
  • When you delete your account or ask for erasure, we delete your personal data within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days, except data we must keep by law, such as tax and accounting records, records of complaints and disputes, and the logs we must retain under the DPDP Rules.
  • Where retained, data is kept only for the legally required period and used only for that purpose.

9. Security & Breach Notification

We use reasonable security safeguards, including encryption of data in transit and sensitive data at rest, access controls limited to staff who need the data, logging and monitoring, and backups. No system is completely secure, so please protect your login details and OTPs. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10. Your Rights

As a Data Principal you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process, the processing we do, and the other parties we have shared it with;
  • Correct, complete or update your data;
  • Erase your data, subject to legal retention requirements;
  • Withdraw consent at any time;
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us (Section 13); and
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

To exercise a right, use the in-app options, our deletion pages (YardStore / YardBuy) or write to grievance.officer@spiralnetworks.in. We may verify your identity first. We aim to respond within 15 days and in any case within the time prescribed by law.

You also have duties under the DPDP Act: give accurate information, do not impersonate anyone, and do not file false or frivolous complaints.

11. Children

The Services are not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data of children, and we do not track or target children with advertising. If we learn that we hold a child's data, we will delete the account and data. Parents or guardians can write to us at the address in Section 13.

12. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for essential functions and, where you allow it, for analytics. The apps use device identifiers and analytics tools to understand usage and fix crashes. You can manage cookies through your browser and permissions through your device settings.

13. Contact, Grievances & Complaints

For any question or complaint about your personal data, write to Kashi Nath Saw, Director, Aspiral Networks Private Limited, at grievance.officer@spiralnetworks.in or the address in Section 1. See the Grievance Redressal page for how we handle complaints and our timelines.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after first using our grievance process.
