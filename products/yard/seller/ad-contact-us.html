How Yard works

Yard is a technology platform that connects local shops with buyers nearby. The sale is between you and your customer.

YardBuy

The buyer app. People near your shop browse your products and place orders.

YardStore

Your seller app. You see the order, accept it, bill it and deliver or keep it ready for pickup.

You get paid

The buyer pays you when the order is delivered or collected. The money never passes through us.

Buyers pay you directly in cash or by UPI. You set your prices, you accept your orders, and you keep 100% of every sale.

Questions shop owners ask

What does it cost?

There is no commission on orders. Sellers pay a simple monthly plan, and the first 100 sellers get the first year free. See the plans and prices.

How do I get paid?

The buyer pays you directly when the order is delivered or collected, in cash or by UPI. Yard does not hold your money.

Can I use it for customers who walk into my shop?

Yes. You can bill walk-in customers, print bills and give them loyalty points, the same as online orders.

Will it work with my thermal printer?

YardStore connects to thermal printers over Bluetooth or LAN. Tell us your printer model on the call and we will help you connect it.

Do I need a computer?

No. The YardStore app runs on Android and iPhone.

What do I need to register?

Your shop details and identity verification. Our team guides you through it on the call.
