Yard Seller App

Seller FAQs

Find answers to the most common questions about using the Yard Seller App.

1. Getting Started
Q1. Who can register as a seller on Yard?

Any Kirana store, local shop, or small/medium retailer in India can register and start selling online.

Q2. How do I install the Yard Seller App?

You can download the app from the Downloads page or directly from the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

Q3. Is there any onboarding fee?

No, onboarding is completely free. Only subscription plans are chargeable depending on your selection.

2. Verification & Account Setup
Q4. What documents are required for seller KYC?

Aadhar card, PAN card, Bank details, and shop registration certificate details are required for verification.

Q5. How long does seller/shop verification take?

Typically 24–48 hours after document submission.

Q6. Why was my KYC rejected?

Incorrect details, unclear images, or mismatched information can lead to rejection. You may resubmit updated documents.

3. Products & Listings
Q7. How do I add products?

Use the "Add Product" option, upload images, set pricing, and enter details. Our team reviews listings before publishing.

Q8. Why is my product in Unverified status?

All listings undergo manual review to ensure quality and safety. It usually takes up to 24 hours.

Q9. What kind of product images can I upload?

Clear product images, your own photos, or high-quality stock images you have rights to use.

Q10. Are there any restricted items?

Yes. Refer to the Restricted Items page for the full list.

4. Orders & Delivery
Q11. How will I receive new order notifications?

You receive in-app notifications and can view orders in your dashboard.

Q12. Who handles delivery?

Delivery or pickup is arranged by you.

Q13. Can I cancel an order?

Sellers may cancel orders only under valid reasons, such as stock unavailability.

5. Payments & Points
Q14. How do I receive payments?

Buyers pay you directly when the order is delivered or collected. Yard does not collect or hold order payments.

Q15. How do membership points work?

Set the points buyers earn on order value, the minimum order value to earn, the rupee discount each point gives on a later order, and the maximum points allowed per order.

Q16. Does Yard charge commission?

No.

6. Fees & Subscriptions
Q17. What fees do sellers pay?

Sellers pay only the subscription fee plus GST for the plan they choose.

Q18. Do buyers pay any fees?

No.

Q19. Are there hidden charges?

No. All fees are transparent and displayed clearly in the Pricing section.

7. Account & Support
Q20. Can I change my shop details?

Yes, you can update shop information in the settings menu. Some changes require re-verification.

Q21. How do I contact support?

Use the "Support" section in the app or contact us through the email: yard-services@spiralnetworks.in

Q22. Can my seller account be suspended?

Yes, for repeated policy violations, restricted items, or fraudulent activity.

8. Chat, Promotions, Invoices & Complaints
Q23. Can I chat with buyers?

Yes. Use the order chat to talk to a buyer about an order and to share photos or files. Please use chat only for order matters, and not for spam or unsolicited promotions.

Q24. Can I send offers to my customers?

Yes. You can send promotional push notifications to buyers who have ordered from you before and have opted in. Buyers can switch promotions off at any time. Your messages must be truthful and follow our Terms.

Q25. What is the return window for orders?

Buyers can report a problem within 24 hours of delivery or pickup. You must take back, replace or refund items that are defective, damaged, expired, wrong, missing or not as described, and orders delivered late (other than for force majeure). Refunds are due within 7 days of approval. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Q26. Who issues invoices to buyers?

Yard generates an invoice on your behalf for each completed order and emails it to the buyer. You remain the supplier, so you are responsible for giving correct business and GST details and for your tax filings.

Q27. Do I need a grievance officer for my shop?

Yes. Sellers on an online marketplace must appoint a grievance officer (the shop owner can do this). Give us the officer's name, contact number and designation, and we show it to buyers. You must acknowledge buyer complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within one month.

Q28. How do I make a complaint to Yard?

Write to our Grievance Officer at grievance.officer@spiralnetworks.in or see the Grievance Redressal page. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.
