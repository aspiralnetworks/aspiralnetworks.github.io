1. Introduction

This Cancellation & Refund Policy explains how cancellations, returns and refunds work for Orders placed through the Yard Buyer App (YardBuy), the Yard Seller App (YardStore) and our websites, operated by Aspiral Networks Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us"). It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

We are a technology platform that connects Buyers with independent Sellers. We do not own, stock or sell products, and we are not a party to the sale between Buyer and Seller.

2. How Payment Works

Buyers pay Sellers directly when the Order is delivered or collected, for example in cash or by UPI, as accepted by the Seller. You can confirm the accepted method with the Seller in the Order chat. We do not collect, hold or refund payments for Orders. Because of this, any refund of money paid for an Order is made by the Seller to the Buyer, and we help resolve the matter if it is not.

3. Order Cancellations

3.1 By the Buyer

  • You can cancel an Order in the app at any time before the Seller accepts it, with no charge.
  • After the Seller accepts, you can ask the Seller to cancel through the app or chat. The Seller should agree unless it has already prepared, packed or dispatched the Order, and should tell you the reason if it cannot.
  • Perishable or made-to-order items may not be cancellable once preparation has started. This is shown on the Listing.
  • We do not charge any cancellation fee. A Seller must not charge you one unless it is a reasonable charge shown to you before you ordered.

3.2 By the Seller

A Seller may cancel an Order for a valid reason, such as an item being out of stock, a pricing error, an unserviceable address or a suspected fraud, and must tell the Buyer promptly. Repeated or unjustified cancellations may lead to lower visibility or account action.

3.3 By the Company

We may cancel an Order for suspected fraud, prohibited items, technical errors or legal reasons. Where this happens and the Buyer has already paid, the Seller must return the money in full.

4. Returns & Replacements

This Policy applies to all Orders. A Seller must take back goods, or replace or refund, where an item is:

  • defective, damaged or expired;
  • wrong, incomplete or missing items; or
  • materially different from the Listing, or delivered late (other than by force majeure).

Return window: please report any problem within 24 hours of delivery or pickup, using "Help" on the Order. This does not limit your rights under law, or any warranty, for defects that could not reasonably be found within 24 hours.

Evidence: please send clear photos or video through the app or chat. Keep the item and its packaging until the issue is resolved.

Not covered (unless the Seller agrees to it): change of mind, damage caused by misuse, used items, and opened items that cannot be resold for hygiene or safety reasons, where this is stated on the Listing.

5. Refunds to Buyers

  • Once a refund is agreed or due, the Seller must refund the amount you actually paid, by the same method you used or another method you accept, within 7 days.
  • A Seller must not refuse a refund that is due under Section 4.
  • If the Seller does not respond or does not refund, raise it as described in Section 7. We will contact the Seller and may reduce its visibility, suspend or remove it from the platform. Because we do not hold the money, we cannot pay the refund ourselves, but you can also use your legal remedies, including a complaint to the consumer commission.

6. Membership Points on Cancellations & Returns

Unless a Seller's published rules are more favourable to you:

  • Points earned on an Order are removed if the Order is cancelled or fully returned, and reduced in proportion for a partial return.
  • Points redeemed on an Order are restored to your balance if the Order is cancelled before fulfilment or fully returned, and restored in proportion for a partial return.
  • The refund amount is the amount you actually paid after the points discount. Points have no cash value and are not refunded as money.

7. How to Raise an Issue

  • Step 1: message the Seller through the Order chat, on the Order in the app.
  • Step 2: if the Seller does not resolve it, use "Help" on the Order in the app or write to yard-services@spiralnetworks.in with the Order details.
  • Step 3: escalate to our Grievance Officer, as described on the Grievance Redressal page. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

8. Seller Subscription Fees

(For Yard Seller App users)

  • Subscription fees are charged for the plan you choose, as shown on the Pricing page, plus applicable GST.
  • Fees are non-refundable for the paid period, regardless of usage, except where (a) you were charged twice or in error, (b) we did not provide the paid service, or (c) the law requires a refund. Report billing errors within 15 days of the charge.
  • You can cancel or downgrade at any time. The change takes effect at the end of the paid billing cycle and no pro-rata refund is given.

9. Seller Account Closure

  • Before closing, a Seller must fulfil or cancel pending Orders and settle any refunds it owes Buyers.
  • A Seller who closes its account should give Buyers at least 15 days' notice if it runs a membership points programme.
  • We keep certain records after closure only as the law requires, as described in our Privacy Policy.

10. Fraud & Misuse

Fake Orders or cancellations, misrepresentation of goods, abuse of membership points, refusal to give a refund that is due, and sale of prohibited items are not allowed. We may cancel Orders, remove points, restrict or suspend accounts, and report fraud to authorities where required by law.

11. Our Role & Your Rights

We are not responsible for the quality, delivery or refund of products sold by Sellers, but we do operate a grievance process and will act on Sellers who do not follow this Policy. Nothing in this Policy limits any right you have as a consumer under applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. For material changes we will give at least 15 days' notice in the app, by email or on this page. Orders placed before a change are handled under the Policy in force when they were placed.
